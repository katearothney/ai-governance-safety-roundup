# AI Governance and Safety Radar

What is actually changing in AI governance, safety, and the market right now, and what does it mean for how you position, message, build, and regulate a product inside it?

This repo is two things. A **framework** other marketers, PMs, and policy people can reuse to track this space, and a **live newsletter** generated from it. Use the framework to stay oriented, read the editions to stay current.

## Who it is for

- **Product marketers**, who need the positioning read: what changed for the buyer and the seller, and where governance is becoming a reason to buy.
- **Product managers**, who need to know what is now table stakes and what is a real differentiator.
- **Policy and governance leads at companies**, who need to see the rules coming before they cost money.

## The rule the whole thing runs on

Every number and every quote gets a link. Where I am reading tea leaves, I say so in the sentence. A briefing you can't check is just vibes with a date on it. If a call looks wrong to you, open an issue and tell me why.
