# Compete and whitespace

*Last updated: August 7, 2026.*

Who wins right now, why, and where the open lanes are.

## The five forces
Read every company against these. Raw model quality is only one of them, and the least contested.

1. **Cost to serve.** With five labs within a few points on quality, price is the live wire. Who delivers good-enough output cheapest, and who owns chips to defend the margin.
2. **Distribution.** Who can put a model in front of people without asking them to go anywhere. Google through Search and Android, Microsoft through Office, OpenAI through the ChatGPT habit.
3. **The governance moat.** Who can hand a regulated buyer a clean compliance story. The newest force and the one moving fastest.
4. **Agents.** Who can move from chat to finished work a company will pay a salary-sized price for.
5. **Openness and ecosystem.** Open weights set the price floor and win developers, closed models bet on capability and control.

## The whitespace
The open lanes sit where the forces are least contested and no one has planted a flag yet.

- **The buyer-ready compliance kit.** Everyone can claim governance. Almost no one hands the buyer a kit that maps a deployment to NIST and ISO in an afternoon, with the evidence attached. Whoever makes governance easy to buy, not just easy to claim, takes the lane.
- **Governance-grade observability.** The observability vendors sell debugging. The open lane is observability built to produce audit evidence a regulator will accept, tied to the frameworks buyers are being measured against.
- **Sovereign deployment stories.** As sovereignty becomes procurement, the vendors with a clean "your data, your tenant, your governance, in-country" story win deals the hyperscalers can't.
- **SMB-friendly compliance.** The EU rules are written for the enterprise. The mid-market has the same obligations and none of the legal budget, and nobody is really serving them yet.

## The read
The contest has moved off the model and onto the operating layer around it. The companies that win the next year are the ones that ride the price-and-agents wave while giving buyers a clean, provable answer on trust. Whoever turns governance from a talking point into a product takes the whitespace.
