# The trust, safety, and security market

**The trend.** The tooling that watches, filters, and stress-tests models has turned from a research hobby into a funded market of its own.

**The evidence.** Gray Swan raised 40 million dollars for red-teaming, Mistral shipped an open safety classifier, and NVIDIA launched a 100-plus-partner security alliance, all in one stretch. Fresh capital is flowing across evals, interpretability, guardrails, observability, and GRC now that the EU AI Act made governance a line item. ([Technical.ly](https://technical.ly/entrepreneurship/gray-swan-ai-security-40m-series-a/), [NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/open-secure-ai-alliance/))

**Why it matters.** This is a market to track on its own, not a footnote to the model market. For PMs it is the roadmap preview, and for PMMs it is where the next category-defining messaging gets written, because a startup that just raised is about to spend on positioning.

**Where it is heading.** Watch the security giants keep acquiring (Protect AI went to Palo Alto Networks, Robust Intelligence to Cisco), and watch observability produce the next breakout.
