# Guardrails

**The trend.** Guardrails, the runtime filtering of what a model takes in and puts out, turned from a research feature into a shipped product across the stack.

**The evidence.** Mistral shipped ShieldStral, a small open-weights safety classifier, and NVIDIA launched the Open Secure AI Alliance with more than 100 partners to build open safety and security tooling. The clouds already ship guardrails as managed services (Bedrock Guardrails, Azure AI Content Safety, Model Armor). ([Mistral](https://mistral.ai/news/shieldstral), [NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/open-secure-ai-alliance/))

**Why it matters.** Guardrails are becoming table stakes for enterprise deployment, which means they stop being a differentiator and start being a checkbox. The differentiation moves to how well they work and how easily a buyer can prove they are on.

**Where it is heading.** Watch open guardrail models pressuring the paid ones, and watch guardrails merge with observability so the same layer both blocks and records.
