# Safety versus speed

**The trend.** The pressure to ship is straining safety commitments, and the industry is quietly resetting how much safety it will trade for pace.

**The evidence.** Anthropic's Responsible Scaling Policy reached version 3.0 and dropped its original promise to not train a model unless safety was shown first, with the reasoning that a unilateral pause just hands the lead to someone less careful. In the same stretch the US finalized voluntary safety tests rather than hard rules, and its safety-agency chief resigned. ([TIME](https://time.com/7380854/exclusive-anthropic-drops-flagship-safety-pledge/), [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/us-finalizes-voluntary-ai-safety-tests-white-house-official-says-2026-08-03/))

**Why it matters.** For a safety-branded company, this is the sharpest test of whether the brand is substance or positioning. Buyers are starting to probe whether safety claims bend under competitive pressure, which makes "here is what we will not do, even to win" a real differentiator.

**Where it is heading.** Watch whether any lab holds a hard line when it costs them a release, and whether regulators fill the gap the voluntary approach leaves.
