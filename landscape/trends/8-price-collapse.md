# The price collapse

**The trend.** The price of frontier-class model output is falling fast, and model access is no longer the moat.

**The evidence.** Frontier output fell to roughly half its cost in about a year, and the July 2026 releases led with price and efficiency, not benchmark supremacy. Cheap open models out of China and Meta keep dragging the paid tier down. ([LLM Stats](https://llm-stats.com/ai-news))

**Why it matters.** With five labs within a few points on quality and all cutting price, "ours is smartest" is a weak message. The differentiation is moving to cost-to-serve, distribution, and the layer around the model. For a seller, the pitch is good-enough-and-cheaper, defended by owned infrastructure where you have it.

**Where it is heading.** Watch whether the halving continues or the labs quietly raise agent pricing once agent products get sticky.
