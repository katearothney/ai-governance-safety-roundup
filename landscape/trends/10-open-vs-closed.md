# Open versus closed

**The trend.** The split between open-weight and closed models is hardening into two different strategies, and it shapes price, developer loyalty, and governance posture.

**The evidence.** Open models (Meta's Llama, DeepSeek, Alibaba's Qwen, and now safety tooling like Mistral's ShieldStral under Apache 2.0) set the price floor and win developers. Closed labs bet on capability and control. On the EU rules, the split showed again: most signed the Code of Practice while Meta, the open flagship, refused. ([Mistral](https://mistral.ai/news/shieldstral), [EU AI Act](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/introduction-to-code-of-practice/))

**Why it matters.** Which side a company picks shapes everything downstream: pricing power, who its champions are, and how it argues about regulation. Open weights are also becoming a governance argument, "you can inspect and run it yourself," not just a cost argument.

**Where it is heading.** Watch whether open safety tooling (classifiers, evals, guardrails) commoditizes the paid trust layer the way open models commoditized the paid model layer.
