# Observability

**The trend.** Watching what agents actually do in production, evals, tracing, monitoring, and optimization, is becoming the operational layer of governance, security, safety, and responsible AI at once.

**The evidence.** Red Hat laid out what a production-grade agent observability stack looks like: tracing every reasoning step, scoring outputs with an LLM judge, adversarially scanning agents before deploy, and turning it all into evidence you can show a regulator. The vendor layer (Arize, Fiddler, WhyLabs, Galileo, LangSmith) is filling in, though no breakout raise has landed yet. ([Red Hat](https://www.redhat.com/en/blog/ai-agent-observability-building-production-grade-operational-layer))

**Why it matters.** Observability is where compliance stops being an assertion and becomes proof. For a regulated buyer, "here is what the agent considered, which tools it called, and what it produced" is the difference between a claim and an audit trail.

**Where it is heading.** This is the lane I expect to move next. Watch for the first big observability raise or acquisition, and for observability built for governance evidence rather than just debugging.
