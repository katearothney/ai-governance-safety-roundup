# Startups (Series D and below)

*Rolling, past two weeks. Last updated: August 7, 2026.*

The early and mid-stage companies building the trust layer, across governance, observability, evals, agent frameworks, AI safety, and responsible AI. This is where the next table-stakes features get invented, so it is worth tracking as its own market. Funding figures are verified where linked and flagged to-verify otherwise.

## This cycle's move
- **Gray Swan AI raised a 40 million dollar Series A.** Led by Wing and Madrona, with 20-plus customers including Snowflake, for model and agent red-teaming. Model stress-testing is now a funded category, not a research hobby. ([Technical.ly](https://technical.ly/entrepreneurship/gray-swan-ai-security-40m-series-a/))

## The categories to track
- **Evaluation and red-teaming.** Gray Swan, Haize Labs, Patronus AI, plus the research shops Apollo Research and METR. Breaking or scoring a model before an attacker or regulator does.
- **Interpretability.** Goodfire (commercial) and Transluce (nonprofit). Looking inside the model, the hardest and most credible corner.
- **Guardrails and runtime security.** Lakera, HiddenLayer, Prompt Security. Filtering inputs and outputs and defending deployed models. Protect AI (Palo Alto Networks) and Robust Intelligence (Cisco) already exited, a sign the security giants see this as core.
- **Observability.** Arize, Fiddler, WhyLabs, Galileo, and LangSmith for watching what agents do in production. No breakout raise yet, and it is the lane I expect to move next. ([Red Hat on the stack](https://www.redhat.com/en/blog/ai-agent-observability-building-production-grade-operational-layer))
- **Agent frameworks.** LangChain and the MCP and A2A interoperability layer, where orchestration is becoming the platform battle.
- **Governance and GRC.** Credo AI and Holistic AI, the paperwork layer that maps a deployment to a regulation.

## What it means
For PMs, the startup layer is the roadmap preview. Evals, tracing, and guardrails are moving from nice-to-have to expected-in-enterprise-deals inside a year. For PMMs, a startup that just raised is about to spend that money on category-defining messaging, so their positioning is worth reading closely.
