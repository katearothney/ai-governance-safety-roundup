# Sources

This is the part I use most. It is the map of where I pull intel from, split by who each outlet is really writing for. When I read the same story in three of these at once, that is my signal it matters.

I read across audiences on purpose. The business press tells me what leaders are being told to think. The developer press tells me what is actually true a few weeks earlier. The policy press tells me what is about to become a constraint. Reading only one of the three is how a product marketer gets surprised.

## For business audiences and leaders
What the people signing the checks are reading.

- **The Information**: the deepest tech and AI scoops, subscription. https://www.theinformation.com/
- **Bloomberg Technology**: markets, deals, and the money behind the models. https://www.bloomberg.com/technology
- **Financial Times**: global business framing, strong on the EU angle. https://www.ft.com/artificial-intelligence
- **The Wall Street Journal**: enterprise and CIO-level coverage. https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai
- **Reuters Technology**: fast, neutral, wire-service reliability. https://www.reuters.com/technology/
- **CNBC**: the market reaction in near real time. https://www.cnbc.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/
- **The Economist**: the slower, structural read. https://www.economist.com/
- **Axios (Ina Fried, "Axios AI+")**: short, sharp, leader-friendly. https://www.axios.com/technology
- **Fortune ("Eye on AI," Jeremy Kahn)**: enterprise AI adoption. https://fortune.com/section/eye-on-ai/
- **Semafor Technology (Reed Albergotti)**: scoops with a business lens. https://www.semafor.com/technology
- **Stratechery (Ben Thompson)**: the best strategy analysis in tech, subscription. https://stratechery.com/
- **MIT Technology Review ("The Algorithm")**: bridges business and technical. https://www.technologyreview.com/

## For developers and technical readers
What is actually true, usually earlier than the business press catches it.

- **TechCrunch**: startup and funding news. https://techcrunch.com/category/artificial-intelligence/
- **The Verge**: product launches and consumer AI. https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence
- **Ars Technica**: technical depth without the hype. https://arstechnica.com/ai/
- **VentureBeat AI**: enterprise and developer tooling. https://venturebeat.com/category/ai/

- **Interconnects (Nathan Lambert)**: sharp on models, training, and open weights. https://www.interconnects.ai/
- **Simon Willison's blog**: hands-on, links-heavy, fast. https://simonwillison.net/

## For governance, policy, legal, and compliance
What is about to become a constraint on the business.

- **Reuters Legal / regulatory**: reliable on enforcement and rulings. https://www.reuters.com/legal/
- **Politico ("Digital Future Daily," and "AI: Decoded" in the EU)**: the best on what regulators are actually doing. https://www.politico.com/technology
- **Lawfare**: AI, national security, and the legal fine print. https://www.lawfaremedia.org/
- **Tech Policy Press**: policy analysis from inside the debate. https://www.techpolicy.press/
- **IAPP**: the privacy and AI-governance profession's home base. https://iapp.org/
- **Just Security**: legal and policy analysis. https://www.justsecurity.org/
- **Law-firm client alerts**: the fastest read on new rules: Gibson Dunn, Latham & Watkins, Holland & Knight, WilmerHale, Hogan Lovells. Free, and written for compliance officers.

## Regulatory and standards bodies
The people who actually write and enforce the rules, and the advisory groups that shape them. Go to the primary source before the secondary coverage.

Government and intergovernmental:

- **European Commission, AI Office (DG CNECT)**: the EU AI Act, at the source. https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai
- **NIST (US)**: the AI Risk Management Framework, the closest thing the US has to a standard. https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
- **US FTC**: enforcement on AI claims and consumer harm. https://www.ftc.gov/
- **OECD.AI Policy Observatory**: the global map of national AI policies. https://oecd.ai/
- **UNESCO**: the Recommendation on the Ethics of AI, the widest-adopted global norm. https://www.unesco.org/en/artificial-intelligence
- **Council of Europe**: the AI treaty, the first binding international one. https://www.coe.int/en/web/artificial-intelligence

National AI safety institutes. A fast-growing network of government bodies that test frontier models and set safety standards, now loosely coordinated through an international network. This is where a lot of the real technical governance work is happening.

- **Canadian AI Safety Institute (CAISI)**: Canada's institute, housed under ISED. https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/canadian-artificial-intelligence-safety-institute
- **ISED, Canada's AI ecosystem**: the government's own map of Canadian AI funding, programs, and institutions. https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/artificial-intelligence-ecosystem
- **US Center for AI Standards and Innovation (CAISI)**: the US institute at NIST, formerly the US AI Safety Institute. https://www.nist.gov/caisi
- **UK AI Safety Institute**: the most active on frontier-model evaluation. https://www.aisi.gov.uk/
- **Japan AI Safety Institute**: https://aisi.go.jp/
- **International Network of AI Safety Institutes**: the coordinating body across national institutes. https://www.nist.gov/aisi/international-network-ai-safety-institutes

Non-governmental, advisory, and standards:

- **Frontier Model Forum**: the labs' own industry body. https://www.frontiermodelforum.org/
- **Partnership on AI**: multi-stakeholder practice and norms. https://partnershiponai.org/
- **MLCommons (AILuminate)**: an industry safety benchmark. https://mlcommons.org/
- **ISO/IEC 42001**: the international AI management-system standard. https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html
- **CSET (Georgetown)**: security and emerging-tech policy research. https://cset.georgetown.edu/
- **Ada Lovelace Institute**: public-interest research on AI and society. https://www.adalovelaceinstitute.org/
- **GovAI (Centre for the Governance of AI)**: academic governance research. https://www.governance.ai/

Civil society and the AI safety community. The non-profits, field-builders, and community hubs that push on safety from outside government and industry. The Canadian ones matter extra from where I sit.

- **AI Safety Canada**: a Canadian hub for the AI safety community. https://aisafety.ca/
- **AI Governance & Safety Canada (AIGS)**: Canadian non-profit on AI governance and safety. https://aigs.ca/
- **Future of Life Institute**: policy advocacy and the group behind several open letters. https://futureoflife.org/
- **Center for AI Safety (CAIS)**: research and field-building, ran the "extinction risk" statement. https://www.safe.ai/
- **BlueDot Impact**: the main on-ramp courses into AI safety and governance careers. https://bluedot.org/
- **80,000 Hours**: career research, a big funnel into the field. https://80000hours.org/
- **Apart Research** and **MATS**: research programs training the next wave of safety researchers.

## Top research universities and academic labs
Where the research comes from before it becomes a product, and where a lot of the safety and policy thinking lives. The Canadian trio matters extra from where I sit.

- **Stanford**: HAI for policy and the AI Index, SAIL for the research. https://hai.stanford.edu/
- **MIT**: CSAIL, plus the Schwarzman College of Computing. https://www.csail.mit.edu/
- **UC Berkeley**: BAIR for research, and the Center for Human-Compatible AI (CHAI) for safety. https://bair.berkeley.edu/
- **Carnegie Mellon**: deep bench across ML and AI security. https://www.cmu.edu/
- **Mila (Montreal)**: Yoshua Bengio's institute, a global center for both capability and safety. https://mila.quebec/en
- **Vector Institute (Toronto)**: the anchor of Canada's AI research. https://vectorinstitute.ai/
- **Amii (Alberta)**: the third of Canada's national AI institutes. https://www.amii.ca/
- **CIFAR**: the research organization behind Canada's Pan-Canadian AI Strategy, and the connective tissue across Mila, Vector, and Amii. https://cifar.ca/
- **Alan Turing Institute (UK)**: the UK's national institute for data science and AI, a peer to Vector and Mila. https://www.turing.ac.uk/
- **Allen Institute for AI (AI2)**: Seattle, strong on open models and research. https://allenai.org/
- **Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems**: the European research anchor. https://is.mpg.de/
- **University of Oxford**: the Oxford Internet Institute and AI governance research. https://www.oii.ox.ac.uk/
- **University of Cambridge**: the Leverhulme Centre for the Future of Intelligence. http://lcfi.ac.uk/
- **Princeton**: the Center for Information Technology Policy (CITP). https://citp.princeton.edu/
- **ETH Zurich**: the strongest European technical AI research. https://ai.ethz.ch/

## Analysis, data, and research reports
For the quantitative backbone. When I need a number nobody can wave away, these are where it comes from: analyst reports, adoption studies, surveys, and hard usage data.

- **Stanford HAI AI Index**: the single best annual data set on the field. https://aiindex.stanford.edu/
- **McKinsey, State of AI**: the most-cited enterprise adoption survey. https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/quantumblack/our-insights
- **Menlo Ventures, State of Generative AI in the Enterprise**: real enterprise spend and vendor share. https://menlovc.com/perspective/
- **Deloitte, State of Generative AI in the Enterprise**: quarterly executive survey data. https://www2.deloitte.com/us/en/pages/consulting/articles/state-of-generative-ai-in-enterprise.html
- **Ramp AI Index**: actual corporate spend on AI tools, from card data. https://ramp.com/ai-index
- **Epoch AI**: the hard data on compute, model scale, and training trends. https://epoch.ai/
- **Artificial Analysis**: independent model performance and price benchmarking. https://artificialanalysis.ai/
- **Gartner**: forecasts and the hype cycle, for the exec-facing framing. https://www.gartner.com/en/information-technology
- **Pew Research Center**: public and worker attitudes toward AI, the human-impact numbers. https://www.pewresearch.org/topic/internet-technology/artificial-intelligence/
