# Qualitative: what they said

*Last updated: August 7, 2026.*

The quotes and positioning lines. What people actually said, attributed and dated, so you can quote them cleanly.

## On safety under competitive pressure
> "It wouldn't actually help anyone for us to stop training AI models."

Jared Kaplan, Chief Science Officer, Anthropic, on why the Responsible Scaling Policy dropped its halt pledge. ([TIME](https://time.com/7380854/exclusive-anthropic-drops-flagship-safety-pledge/))

## On trust and sovereignty
> "By building trust, supporting Canadian talent and upholding our values, we are positioning Canada as a global leader in responsible and inclusive AI."

Minister Evan Solomon, on Canada's national AI strategy. ([Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/innovation-science-economic-development/news/2026/06/minister-solomon-highlights-canadas-national-artificial-intelligence.html))

## On Europe holding its line
> "People can trust our technologies."

Henna Virkkunen, EU Executive Vice-President for Tech Sovereignty, on the five-year goal for European AI, chips, and cybersecurity. ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/12/5-takeaways-europe-tech-chief-henna-virkkunen-ai))

## On what the EU did not blink on
> "Article 50 transparency obligations for AI systems largely remain on the original schedule."

The Digital Omnibus write-up, on the one obligation that did not get postponed. ([Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/))

## How to use these
A positioning line is data. Watch the verbs. "Welcomes a rule" and publishes a readiness guide is a company claiming the compliance high ground. "Opposes a rule" and publishes a critique is a company betting speed matters more to its buyers. Neither is neutral.
