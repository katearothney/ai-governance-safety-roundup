# Data

The evidence backbone, split two ways.

- [**quant.md**](quant.md), the numbers worth citing in a deck or memo, each with a source, plus a "pull each cycle" list for the fast-moving market figures.
- [**qualitative.md**](qualitative.md), the quotes and positioning lines. What the people actually said, attributed and dated.

Nothing here is invented. Where a figure is an estimate rather than a hard number, it says so.
