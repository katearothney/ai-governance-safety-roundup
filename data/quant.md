# Quant: the numbers

*Last updated: August 7, 2026.*

The durable figures worth citing, each linked. The per-edition stat pack in the [newsletter](../newsletter/) catches the fresh ones. This keeps the ones that stay useful.

## Regulation and enforcement
- **Up to 35M euros or 7% of global annual turnover.** Top EU AI Act fine tier, for prohibited practices. Other violations run to 15M euros or 3 percent, misleading regulators to 7.5M euros or 1 percent. ([Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/04/us-companies-face-eu-ai-acts-possible-august-2026-compliance-deadline))
- **December 2, 2027 and August 2, 2028.** The postponed high-risk deadlines, standalone systems then AI in regulated products. ([Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/))
- **About 26 signed, Meta out.** The EU GPAI Code of Practice count, the clearest map of who is betting on compliance. ([EU AI Act](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/introduction-to-code-of-practice/))
- **10^25 FLOP.** The training-compute threshold above which the Code's safety chapter bites, currently about 5 to 15 companies. ([EU AI Act](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/introduction-to-code-of-practice/))

## Model economics
- **Around half the price.** The fall in frontier-class output cost in about a year, from positioning rather than a published figure. ([LLM Stats](https://llm-stats.com/ai-news))
- **600B-plus parameters.** Meta's open-weight Llama 5 flagship. ([LLM Stats](https://llm-stats.com/ai-news))
- **3B parameters, one 16GB GPU, Apache 2.0.** Mistral's ShieldStral safety classifier. ([Mistral](https://mistral.ai/news/shieldstral))

## Safety, incidents, and funding
- **10 of 122 runs.** UK safety-test runs where agents took unsanctioned real-world actions, contained in about an hour. ([AISI](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/blog/incident-report-unsanctioned-agent-behaviour-during-cyber-testing))
- **40 million dollars.** Gray Swan AI Series A for model and agent red-teaming. ([Technical.ly](https://technical.ly/entrepreneurship/gray-swan-ai-security-40m-series-a/))
- **Up to 200 million dollars.** Anthropic's two-year US Department of Defense prototype agreement. ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-and-the-department-of-defense-to-advance-responsible-ai-in-defense-operations))
- **100-plus partners.** Founding members of NVIDIA's Open Secure AI Alliance. ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/open-secure-ai-alliance/))
