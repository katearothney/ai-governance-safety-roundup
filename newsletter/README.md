# Newsletter: the tool come to life

This is where the framework becomes a product. Each edition of **Governance Signals** is generated from the [landscape](../landscape/) and [data](../data/) sections and published here as email-ready HTML, in a clean executive format you can send or paste into a newsletter tool.

If the rest of the repo is the reusable structure, this folder is the showcase: proof the tool produces something people actually want to read.

## Editions

- [Governance Signals, August 2026: From the Model Race to the Governance Moat](2026-08-governance-signals.html)

## How an edition is made

1. The [framework](../README.md) holds the current material: rolling [news](../landscape/news/), evolving [trends](../landscape/trends/), the [compete and whitespace](../landscape/compete-whitespace.md) read, and the [data](../data/) backbone.
2. The `market-radar-roundup` skill synthesizes that material into an edition: an executive summary, recommendations, a leadership POV, market signals, competitive dynamics, trends, and a stat pack.
3. The edition ships here as a self-contained HTML file, email-ready, every claim linked.

## Format

Each edition follows the same shape, tuned for product marketers, product managers, and policy leads:

- **Cover and executive summary**: the one thesis for the cycle.
- **Recommendations**: what to do about it this cycle.
- **Leadership POV**: the quotes that frame the moment.
- **Market signals**: what moved, sourced.
- **Competitive dynamics**: the per-company read.
- **Trends**: the durable throughlines.
- **Compete and whitespace**: where the open lanes are.
- **Stat pack**: verified numbers to pull.
